About Me

🎤 Singing 🚲 Biking

I'm a graphic design student who's just getting started, and I'm genuinely excited about it. Design, to me, is less about getting everything perfect right away and more about staying curious — trying new tools, new styles, and new ideas, and learning something from each one.

Outside of design, you'll usually find me singing when I need to unwind, or out biking when I want some fresh air and a clear head. Both of those, honestly, end up feeding back into how I create — a good melody or a nice ride can spark an idea just as easily as a mood board can.

I'm still building my portfolio and my skills, and I'm perfectly okay with that — I'd rather grow in the open than wait until everything looks "finished." So consider this page a running work in progress, just like me.
